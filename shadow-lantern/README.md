# Shadow Lantern

A six-level Godot 4.7.2 puzzle prototype about moving lights, casting shadows, and walking through the shadow world.

## Controls

- Reality: drag lamps with the mouse, or Tab to select and Left/Right to move one
- Space / Xbox A: enter or leave the shadow world
- Shadow world: Left/Right or A/D to walk
- G / Xbox Y: grow a magic plant
- Enter: activate the final boss seal
- R: restart level

Shadows are real platforms. Arrange the lights so object shadows bridge each gap, then enter the shadow world and cross to the door.
